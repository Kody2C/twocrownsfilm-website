The Studio

Two Crowns Film is a video production company founded in 2026 by Kody Trent. We're based in beautiful Lakeland, Florida, perfectly situated for video projects in all of Florida.

No matter what level of production you need, Two Crowns Film is here to make it happen. Our passion lies in artistic storytelling for organizations whose mission lies in their core values and beliefs. We partner with non-profits, advocacy organizations, ministries, campaigns, content creators, and more.

Our Founder

Kody Trent is a filmmaker based in Lakeland, Florida. At the University of Central Florida, he pursued a Film BFA (Narrative Production) degree, and made multiple award winning short films. He was also employed by Turning Point USA from 2019-2022, serving as the social media chair and vice president of TPUSA @ UCF, and a chapter representative. He was a grassroots conservative activist, constantly tabling and attending conferences. For over 3 years after graduation, he worked for Farmore, a video production company in Tampa. His role quickly grew into more than editing, doing cinematography and assistant camera work, and learning the entirety of the production process. He now wishes to re-enter the arena, fighting for his beliefs and values.

Today, his passion lies in documentary work, and helping others emotionally impact their audiences.
